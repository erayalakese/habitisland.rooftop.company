# Faro — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 August 2026

**Last updated:** 17 August 2026

Faro ("the app") is developed and published by Rooftop ("we", "us"). This
policy covers the Faro Android app and the Faro website. It explains what data
stays on your device, what leaves it, why it is used, how long it is kept, and
the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your habit content stays on your device. Habit names, check-ins, consumption
  counts, budgets and progress are not sent to us or our service providers.
- Optional product analytics is off until you choose **Share usage data**.
- Technical crash and performance reports are sent to Sentry so we can keep the
  app reliable. These reports can include an IP address and technical request
  context, but are designed not to include habit content.
- Faro uses anonymous service identifiers to check Faro+ subscription status.
- The website sends analytics only after you accept analytics. If you join the
  waitlist, your email is sent to LaunchList only for the launch announcement.
- No ads, no sale of data and no Faro account are required.

## Data that stays on your device

Faro is offline-first. Everything you enter into it — habit names and types,
daily check-ins, resistance budgets, reduction curves, achievements, island
decorations and notification preferences — is stored in a local database on
your device.

- We have no application server that receives this habit content and no way to
  read it remotely.
- Removing the app removes its live local database. Your device's own backup
  system may retain or restore a personal backup according to settings you
  control and your device provider's terms.
- During one optional onboarding moment Faro can use the microphone to measure
  a live sound level. It does not record audio, keep an audio buffer or send
  microphone data anywhere.
- Home-screen widget data and notification preferences remain on the device.

Habits can concern sensitive topics such as smoking or alcohol. Habit content
is therefore excluded from analytics events, crash-report fields, purchase
records and the community counter described below.

## Data that leaves the app

The app uses the following service providers over encrypted connections.

### 1. Aggregate community counter — Cloudflare

During onboarding, Faro reads a public total showing contributions added to a
shore counter in the previous seven days. If you complete a resistance setup,
the app sends a bodyless request that increases the current day's total.

- The request contains no habit content, user ID, device ID or installation ID.
- Faro's Worker does not read, hash or store the request's IP address.
- All requests share one short-lived global burst-limit bucket; it is not keyed
  to a person, device or IP address.
- Cloudflare necessarily processes ordinary network metadata, including a
  source IP address, to route and protect the HTTPS request. Worker observability
  and invocation-log storage are disabled.
- The only product record is one integer total per UTC calendar day. Those
  anonymous aggregate totals may be retained as historical records; they cannot
  be tied back to a person or device.

Purpose: show an honest aggregate community moment while limiting simple
automated request bursts.

### 2. Usage analytics — Google Firebase Analytics (only with your consent)

If you choose **Share usage data** near the end of onboarding, we collect a
small set of product events such as onboarding completion, habit creation, a
generic check-in, review-request attempts and paywall outcomes. We also receive
general device information (for example device model, OS and app version),
coarse region derived from IP and a random app-instance identifier.

- Events do not include habit names, habit types, consumption counts, budgets,
  reduction curves or other habit content.
- Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled.
- You can turn analytics off at any time under **Usage analytics** in Settings.
  Future analytics collection stops and the Firebase identifier is removed from
  RevenueCat.
- User-level and event-level analytics data is retained for no more than two
  months. Google Analytics standard aggregate reports may not be affected by
  this event-retention setting.

Purpose: understand whether core product flows work and improve the app. If you
decline, Faro does not send Firebase Analytics events.

### 3. Crash and performance reports — Sentry

Faro sends technical error, performance, transaction and log information to
Sentry so we can diagnose failures. Depending on the event, this can include a
stack trace, app and device state, device model, OS and app version, route or
screen breadcrumbs, request headers and the network IP address.

- Faro has no account username, email address or habit profile to attach to a
  Sentry event.
- Habit names, counts, budgets and other habit content are not intentionally
  written to Sentry fields, breadcrumbs, route names or logs.
- Detailed event data is retained for no more than 90 days. We may delete it
  earlier when it is no longer useful.

Purpose: app security, crash diagnosis and performance reliability.

### 4. Purchases — RevenueCat and Google Play Billing

Faro configures RevenueCat with an anonymous app-user identifier so the app can
check whether Faro+ is active and restore access after reinstall. RevenueCat
can receive this identifier, app/device metadata and entitlement state even if
you have not completed a purchase. If you subscribe, Google Play processes the
payment and RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription status such as the
product, purchase time, renewal, cancellation and entitlement state.

If Usage analytics is enabled, Faro also gives RevenueCat the random Firebase
app-instance identifier. This lets RevenueCat attribute subscription lifecycle
and revenue events to the same analytics instance. Turning Usage analytics off
removes that identifier and stops future Firebase collection on the device.

- We do not receive your payment-card or bank details; Google Play processes
  them.
- Purchase and entitlement records are kept as long as needed to provide and
  restore Faro+, meet financial/legal obligations, resolve disputes and prevent
  fraud. Google Play and RevenueCat apply their own retention duties to records
  they control.

Purpose: present the subscription, verify access and restore purchases.

## Website and waitlist

### Optional website analytics — Google Analytics

The Faro website loads Google Analytics only after you select **Accept
analytics**. It can then collect page views, interactions, browser/device
information, coarse region derived from IP, cookies and a random browser/client
identifier. It does not receive app habit content.

The site stores your analytics choice in local storage. If you choose **Only
necessary**, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. User-level and
event-level website analytics is retained for no more than two months; standard
aggregate reports may persist longer.

### Waitlist — LaunchList

If you submit an email address on the website, it is sent to LaunchList, our
waitlist provider. We use it only for Faro's launch announcement, not for an
automated marketing sequence, and do not sell it. Every message provides a way
to unsubscribe.

We delete the waitlist within 30 days after the launch announcement is sent, or
earlier if you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your address. A later newsletter
or marketing sequence would require a new, separate choice.

## What we do not do

- No advertising or advertising SDKs.
- No sale or rental of personal data.
- No marketing profile built from app behaviour.
- No Faro account, password, contacts, precise location, photos or files.
- No audio recording or transmission.
- No habit or health content sent to analytics, crash reporting or purchase
  providers.

## Your choices and rights

- **App analytics:** accept or decline during onboarding; change the choice at
  any time in Settings.
- **Website analytics:** choose **Accept analytics** or **Only necessary** on
  the site. You can clear the site's local storage/cookies to reset the choice.
- **Waitlist:** unsubscribe from the launch email or ask us to delete your
  address.
- **Local habit data:** remove individual records in Faro or remove the app.
  Device backups are controlled through your device settings.
- **Provider data:** contact us to request access, correction or deletion tied
  to an identifier you can provide. Some purchase records may have to be kept
  for legal, accounting, security or dispute purposes.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as
the EU/UK GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK, including access, correction, erasure,
restriction, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority.

## Processors and international transfers

Our providers include Cloudflare, Inc.; Google LLC (Firebase, Google Analytics
and Google Play); Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry); RevenueCat, Inc.; and
LaunchList. They may process data outside your country, including in the United
States, under their contractual safeguards and applicable transfer mechanisms.

## Children

Faro is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child
has provided data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where
applicable.

## Changes to this policy

We update this policy before materially changing what Faro collects or why it
is used. The latest version and its update date will remain on this page.
Material app changes will also be described in release notes or an in-app
notice when appropriate.
