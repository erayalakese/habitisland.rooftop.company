Some progress belongs in the sky.

Faro keeps two kinds of progress separate, so a hard day never erases the life you've already built.

For habits you build Buildings grow from your total check-ins, not consecutive days. Miss one and growth simply pauses. Nothing resets.
For habits you leave behind Weekly seals notice rhythm and recovery. The 30-, 90-, 180-, and 365-day stars mark clean stretches; once earned, they stay lit.

Guided Voyages

When a habit needs a little story.

Faro's guided programs turn real-world practice into small adventures. They ask for movement, water, and a bit of curiosity. Never perfection.

Chapter one

Archipelago Expedition

Start with the smallest movement you can do today. Walking and exercise fill your boat with energy; each Faro day lets you sail one leg farther, from Yelkovan Cove to the Current Gardens and the Dark Lighthouse.

Make it home, and an Expedition Pier remains on your shore. The voyage ends. The movement stays yours.
